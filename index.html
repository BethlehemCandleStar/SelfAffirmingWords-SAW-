SAW AGENT

Daily words shaped around what matters to you.

The SAW Agent uses the focus, tone and reasons you choose to save to create a new daily affirmation. You remain in control of what is stored, regenerated or pinned.

SAW is a motivational wellbeing prototype and is not presented as medical treatment, addiction treatment, emergency support or a replacement for professional care.
